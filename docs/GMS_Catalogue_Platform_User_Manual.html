About this manual

Welcome to the GMS Catalogue Platform

This manual explains the standard workflow from ERP product data to a published digital or PDF catalogue. Menu items are permission-based, so a user may not see every feature shown here.

Important: The platform saves Catalogue Studio work automatically, but publishing, access changes, password resets, and certain master-data updates require an explicit button click.

Getting started

1. Sign in and navigate

  1. Open the platform URL supplied by your administrator.
  2. Enter your registered email address and password.
  3. Select Sign in. The platform opens the first workspace your permissions allow.

Main navigation

The left sidebar groups destinations into Overview, Catalogue Management, Product Management, Promotions, Organization, Access Control, and System. Select Collapse to reduce the sidebar. On mobile, use the menu button to open it.

The header search finds products, catalogues, brands, and categories. Press / when no form field is active to focus it. Use arrow keys and Enter to choose a result.

Security: Never share an account. Sign out when using a shared computer. Contact an administrator after repeated failed sign-in attempts.

2. Use the dashboard

The dashboard presents only actions and metrics allowed by your role.

Quick ActionsStart common tasks such as adding products, opening Catalogue Studio, creating promotions, or synchronizing data.
Requires AttentionOpen filtered queues for missing images, incomplete records, or approval work.
My WorkContinue catalogues, promotions, and review tasks assigned to you.

Status and synchronization indicators link to details when the user has access. A disabled indicator is informational and does not require action.

Product management

3. Manage products and media

Find and review a product

  1. Open Products from the sidebar.
  2. Search by product name, code, barcode, brand, catalogue, or category. Apply status and quality filters when needed.
  3. Select a product row to open its detail panel.
  4. Review ERP source information before changing editable catalogue fields.
  5. Select Save draft or the available workflow action.

Images and videos

  • Upload clear product images with suitable resolution and background.
  • Choose the primary image used by product cards and default catalogue layouts.
  • Use Product Media for centralized image and video management.
  • Videos may be uploaded or linked from an approved external service, depending on permission.
ERP data: Source values may be restored by synchronization. Make catalogue-specific presentation changes in the intended editable fields or Catalogue Studio.
Catalogue management

4. Create and manage catalogues

Use All Catalogues to manage catalogue records, publication status, products, covers, price visibility, and audience links.

Standard workflow

  1. Create or select a catalogue.
  2. Set its title, brand or brand mode, language, audience, validity dates, and price behavior.
  3. Add products and confirm their order.
  4. Add a cover and category presentation settings where required.
  5. Preview the result and correct missing data or media.
  6. Publish only after approval. Copy the appropriate catalogue link for the intended audience.
StatusMeaning
DraftWork is editable and not ready for customers.
In reviewSubmitted for validation or approval.
PublishedApproved output is available through its active share link.
Archived/InactiveRetained for history but removed from the active workflow.
Booklet without prices: When no price option is selected, the catalogue card presents one general Catalog link instead of multiple price-level links.
Design workflow

5. Work in Catalogue Studio

Create a design

  1. Open Catalogue Studio and choose New catalogue.
  2. Select a starting point: blank page, A4 blank image page, system template, personal template, or uploaded template.
  3. Enter a catalogue name and choose the catalogue type.
  4. Select A4 Portrait or A4 Landscape, then open Studio.

Editor essentials

AreaUse
ElementsAdd text, images, logos, shapes, QR codes, tables, grids, barcodes, page numbers, and product cards.
UploadsUpload your own images and drag them onto the page or into grid frames.
PagesAdd, duplicate, reorder, and remove catalogue pages.
LayersChoose overlapping elements and adjust their stacking order.
PropertiesEdit the selected element, crop an image, rotate, flip, resize, or replace media.

Image grids

  1. Add Grids from Elements.
  2. Choose the number of rows and columns.
  3. Drag an uploaded image into each frame.
  4. Select a frame to crop, reposition, rotate, flip, fill, or reset its image.
  5. Use the remove-image action when you want to clear the image while keeping the frame.
Saving: Changes save automatically. Use Save version before a major redesign and Save page/catalogue template when the layout should be reused.
Preview and distribution

6. Preview and share a booklet

Choose Booklet in preview to view pages as a publication. Desktop shows a cover or two-page spread where appropriate. Mobile shows one page at a time.

  • Use the left and right arrows, page thumbnails, keyboard arrows, or swipe gestures.
  • On touch devices, swipe left for the next page and right for the previous page.
  • Use Full screen for the largest reading area.
  • Use Fit or zoom controls when available.
  • Trying to advance after the final page displays a Last Page notification.

Publish and share

  1. Confirm every page in Preview.
  2. Publish the catalogue.
  3. Open the public catalogue link and verify it matches Preview.
  4. Copy the correct audience link or download the PDF.
  5. Test the link in a private browser window before sending it externally.
Audience safety: Different share links may expose different price levels. Always confirm the audience and price label before sharing.
Promotion workflow

7. Manage promotions

  1. Open All Promotions and select the create action.
  2. Enter the name, occasion, schedule, products, pricing, and media.
  3. Save the draft and review it in Preview.
  4. Submit for approval when complete.
  5. An authorized approver reviews, rejects, approves, or publishes the promotion.

Use Promotion Calendar to check schedule conflicts and upcoming campaigns. Promotion Approvals contains work awaiting an authorized decision.

Time zone: Platform schedules use Asia/Bangkok unless a screen explicitly states otherwise.
Account administration

8. Create users and reset passwords

Create a user

  1. Open Users under Organization.
  2. Select + New user.
  3. Enter full name, email address, department, position, optional employee code, team, role, and a temporary password.
  4. Select Create user. The platform creates its internal username automatically from the email address.
  5. Send the temporary password through a secure channel.

Reset a password

  1. Select the user from Platform users.
  2. Open Password and lockout.
  3. Enter a strong replacement password of at least 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol.
  4. Select Reset password. The previous password stops working immediately.

Use Clear lockout after confirming the account owner when failed sign-ins have locked the account.

Access control

9. Assign roles and permissions

Permissions follow a default-deny model. Users receive access through roles, organization assignments, and explicit overrides.

Role permission matrix

  1. Open Organization → Permissions and select a role.
  2. Search or expand a module group.
  3. Enable individual permissions, or use Select all/Clear within a group.
  4. Review the unsaved-change count and select Save permissions.

User-specific access

  • Assign direct roles and global restrictions.
  • Use Select all/Clear all for brands, categories, products, catalogues, and price lists.
  • Add direct exceptions only when a normal role cannot represent the requirement.
  • Review the effective permission simulator before saving.
  • Enter an audit reason. Protected SuperAdmin changes require password confirmation.
Least privilege: Do not grant SuperAdmin for convenience. Prefer the smallest role and data scope that allows the user to complete assigned work.
Master data

10. Configure the organization

The Organization workspace groups Departments, Positions, Teams, Brands, and Permissions.

DepartmentsCreate the organizational hierarchy and active departments.
PositionsAssociate job positions with departments and levels.
TeamsAssign a department and the brands managed by each team.
BrandsMaintain brand codes, descriptions, and active status.
PermissionsDefine permission records and assign them through grouped roles.

Create departments and brands before configuring teams and user data scope. Mark obsolete records inactive when history must be preserved.

Administration

11. System administration

Data synchronization

Review the latest ERP synchronization status before manually running a new sync. Confirm product counts, warnings, and the most recent successful completion time.

Backups and system health

  • Create or download backups only with appropriate authorization.
  • Review CPU, memory, disk, database, API, and failed-login indicators.
  • Use Audit Logs to investigate sensitive changes and access events.
  • Change ERP or general settings only during an approved maintenance process.
Do not experiment in production: Backup, synchronization, settings, and permission changes may affect all users. Record the reason and expected outcome before proceeding.
Help

12. Troubleshooting and daily checklist

ProblemWhat to check
A menu or button is missingYour role may not include the required permission. Ask an administrator to review effective access.
Search shows unexpected textClear the field and refresh. Platform search fields block browser autofill, but an older cached page may require a hard refresh.
Product data changed after syncThe value may be ERP-owned. Confirm whether the field should be changed in ERP or in catalogue presentation data.
Image does not fill a frameSelect the frame and use crop, zoom, position, or Fill frame. Confirm the image finished uploading.
Public catalogue differs from PreviewSave, publish the latest version, refresh the public link, and confirm the correct share token.
Reset password is disabledEnter a password meeting the minimum length and strength requirements.
Export failsConfirm pages render in PDF Preview, retry once, then report the catalogue ID and time of failure.

Before publishing

  • Correct catalogue, brand, language, and audience selected.
  • All pages are ordered correctly and contain no empty unintended frames.
  • Images are sharp, correctly cropped, and licensed for use.
  • Product names, codes, prices, stock, and promotion dates are verified.
  • Desktop, mobile, booklet, fullscreen, and PDF previews are checked as applicable.
  • The correct share link is tested in a private browser window.
When reporting a problem: Include the page URL, catalogue or product ID, expected result, actual result, time of occurrence, browser, and a screenshot. Never include a password.
